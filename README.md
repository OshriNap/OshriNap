I study how AI systems represent information, generate language, and turn reasoning into useful computation. My work connects mathematical ideas with models, experiments, and tools people can explore.

At IBM Research Haifa, I work on multimodal learning and document understanding. I co-authored **Granite Vision** and **REAL-MM-RAG**. My background spans a PhD in Electrical Engineering, distributed optimization, and reinforcement learning.

## Explore my work

### [Token Maturation](https://github.com/OshriNap/Token-maturation)

**What if a language model could refine a token before committing to it?**

An exploration of autoregressive generation in continuous embedding space. A “liquid tail” of token vectors undergoes iterative refinement before discrete projection, separating prediction from commitment.

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.04854) · [Project & generation demos](https://oshrinap.github.io/Token-maturation/)

## Ideas I'm exploring

- **Continuous language generation:** separating the evolution of a representation from the decision to emit a token.
- **Cognitive offloading:** turning repeated AI reasoning into reusable, verifiable computation.
- **Representation and complexity:** understanding what makes a pattern useful to one observer and invisible to another.

I write about these questions and build interactive explanations at **[oshrinaparstek.com](https://oshrinaparstek.com)**.
